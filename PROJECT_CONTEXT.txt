A. PROJECT SUMMARY
======================================================================
- Product Name: AttendWise AI
- Category: Agentic AI-powered Time & Attendance Exception Management & Resolution Platform
- One-Line Pitch: AttendWise AI detects workforce attendance anomalies, investigates them using employee schedules and approved company policies, recommends evidence-backed resolutions, obtains human approval for consequential changes, and safely carries out authorized actions with a complete audit trail.
- Problem Being Solved: Traditional Time & Attendance (T&A) systems flag punch anomalies (missing clock-outs, late arrivals, overtime, conflicting shifts), but investigation and resolution remain tedious, manual, and prone to policy inconsistency. AttendWise AI provides deterministic detection, contextual AI investigation, RAG policy retrieval with verified citations, durable multi-day workflow orchestration, and human-in-the-loop (HITL) approval with atomic transactional execution.
- Target Users and Roles:
  * EMPLOYEE: View own shifts, attendance records, exceptions, submit punch confirmation/dispute evidence.
  * MANAGER: View direct reports' exceptions, review AI investigation & citations, approve/reject/request info.
  * HR_ADMIN: Organization-wide oversight, manage policies and documents, reassign/escalate, view audit trails.
  * SYSTEM_ADMIN: Manage system health, view workflow run failures, re-trigger failed steps.
- Core Value Proposition: Reduces manager exception resolution time by 80% while strictly preventing unauthorized payroll modifications, guaranteeing policy compliance, and ensuring 100% auditability.
- Technical Stack Compliance:
  * Database: SQLite via better-sqlite3 with ACID transactions and raw SQL schema (Prisma strictly excluded per user instructions).
  * Backend: Node.js 22 + Express + TypeScript (Core API), Python 3.12 + FastAPI + Pydantic (AI Service).
  * Frontend: Next.js 15 + React 19 + TailwindCSS + Lucide icons.
  * Orchestration: Inngest durable workflows (human approval pauses, retries) + LangGraph state machine.
  * Validation: Zod (TypeScript) + Pydantic v2 (Python).
  * Vector Store: Qdrant client with dense cosine embeddings and built-in offline grounded policy fallback.

======================================================================
C. ARCHITECTURE DECISIONS (ADRs)
======================================================================
- ADR-001: Inngest as Durable Outer Orchestrator
  * Use Inngest for all long-running workflows, retries, sleeps, and human approval waits (waitForEvent).
- ADR-002: SQLite as ACID Business Source of Truth (No Prisma)
  * Use better-sqlite3 with raw SQL schema migrations and atomic transactions. Fully compliant with approved stack.
- ADR-003: Qdrant & Grounded Fallback for Policy Retrieval
  * Dense vector retrieval with metadata filtering + grounded synthetic policy fallbacks ensuring zero-dependency demo reliability.
- ADR-004: AI Service is Read-Only & Untrusted
  * AI agents cannot mutate database records directly. They produce validated Pydantic structures.
- ADR-005: Consequential Changes Require Human Approval
  * Timecards are never modified automatically without authorized manager or HR sign-off.
