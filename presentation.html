Slide 04 · Reliability & Governance Defensive Engineering

Safety Guardrails, Testing & Verification

Rigorous mathematical testing, read-only AI isolation, and verified citation grounding.

Multi-Tier Safety Guardrails

1. Read-Only AI Agent Boundaries

AI agents are strictly prohibited from writing or adjusting database timecards. They can only emit structured proposals into the approval queue.

2. Anti-Hallucination Citation Verifier

Grok review agent verifies that every recommendation quotes verbatim policy text from ingested documents. Unverified proposals are rejected.

3. Consequential Human Approval & Auto-Expiration

Approval requests automatically expire after 48 hours. Rejections mandate business comments for auditable record-keeping.
